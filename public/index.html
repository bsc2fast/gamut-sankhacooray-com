Gamut

Every scale is a shape. The wheel holds the twelve notes, with C on top. Lines join the notes the scale uses, and microtones sit between the sockets at their true angle. Every sound is synthesised in your browser.

Remix a song: press R or the music button. Load a tune, pick any scale, and hear it move there, major to minor, pop to Hijaz.

Philips Hue: run the local relay from the repo, then press Shift+H. The room takes the scene's colours and flashes with every note.

MIDI keyboard: press M or the keyboard button. Choose how keys outside the scale behave, or let the help fade as you improve.
